Frequently Asked Questions

Does BuildPro replace our external accounting software?

No. BuildPro is an operational cost-governance layer for construction: project budgets, purchase commitments, certified client claims and site expenses. Statutory tax filings and bank feeds stay with your accounting team.

Can different construction companies use the same product?

Yes. BuildPro is architected as a multi-tenant SaaS application. Each company registers an isolated workspace with its own projects, staff, suppliers, and financial records.

Does BuildPro work on phones and tablets at site?

Yes. The Mobile Site Operations module provides large touch-friendly buttons for site engineers to log daily reports, worker attendance, slump test inspections and safety items without needing a laptop.
